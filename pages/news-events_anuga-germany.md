Experience the leading food fair in Cologne,Germany

VISIT US : HALL 1.2/ STAND N017

Anuga 2023, the most renowned trade fair for food and beverage in the food processing and packaging machinery industry, will be staged from the 7th to the 11th of October at the Messe Cologne. The event is expected to gather over 170 000 trade visitors from 201 countries and more than 8000 exhibiting companies from different sectors.

Anuga 2023 has a varied schedule that incorporates practical demonstrations, competitions, and social events. The complementing program will offer a plethora of opportunities for contracting and networking, as well as presentations of the newest technologies. The goal of the event will be to give insight into 12 important product categories:

Delicatessen products, gourmet food, and staple food
Frozen food and ice cream products
Meat, sausage, game, and poultry
Fresh convenience food, delicatessen, fish, fruit & vegetables
Dairy products (milk, cheese, etc.)
Bread, baked goods, and spreads
Drinks
Hot beverages
Technic and equipment for food services and the catering market
Organic products
Free From, Health & Functional Foods
Associations, organizations, trade press, services, IT
The wide spectrum of products and side events have made Anuga a vital source of information and innovations while also keeping an informal and personal ambience. To further raise the value of the event, the participants will be able to partake at two large conference platforms - Newtrition X for product development, nutritional medicine, technology, and sales and the New Food Conference for plant-based and cellular agriculture.
